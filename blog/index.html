Topic selection

A new article needs a new reason to exist

The main TC Lottery pages already cover broad Login, Register, App Download, Bonus, Invite Code, Payments and Not Working intents. A blog article is added only when the topic can answer a narrower question in materially greater depth or when a current external change creates useful context for users.

For example, a general “TC Lottery login problem” belongs on the existing login or problem page. A focused OTP-delivery article can justify its own route because it can separate request generation, registered-number mismatch, resend order, SMS filtering and suspicious-message handling without turning the broad login page into an endless troubleshooting document.

The same rule applies to payments and Android. A detailed UPI reconciliation article is different from the main wallet overview because it maps bank-side and platform-side states. An Android installation article is different from the app download page because it can explain package signatures, target-level compatibility and Play Protect decisions in depth.

No artificial daily quota

The site may publish several fresh articles when several strong topics exist. It will not split one question into multiple near-identical routes only to increase the number of URLs.

Article standard

What a useful TC Lottery blog article should contain

Direct problem definition

The article states what is known, what can be checked locally and what remains controlled by the live platform, bank, telecom network or Android system.

Practical evidence

Tables, status maps, exact records to preserve and least-destructive troubleshooting steps are preferred over repeated promotional wording.

Current verification

Time-sensitive claims are checked against appropriate current references where possible, with the last-checked date visible on the article.

Every article remains connected to the relevant core TC Lottery page through contextual internal links. That lets the broad account page stay stable while the blog handles current or highly specific problems. The blog does not replace platform support for account decisions, payment adjustments or private account recovery.

Update method

Existing articles are updated before a duplicate route is considered

If a topic changes after publication, the first choice is to improve the existing article at the same URL. A changed Android requirement belongs inside the established Android installation article; a new UPI status detail belongs inside the payment-reconciliation article; a new SMS-safety control belongs inside the OTP article when it changes the checks users should perform.

A separate article is justified only when the new event has its own distinct question, evidence and practical outcome. This keeps the blog readable and prevents users from landing on several pages that repeat the same answer with slightly different wording.

Keep the stable URL

Meaningful updates are added to the article that already owns the topic so earlier references and internal links remain useful.

Show the checked date

Time-sensitive articles display when their facts were reviewed. A date is changed only when the content itself has been materially rechecked.

Retire only when necessary

If a topic becomes obsolete, the old route should be handled deliberately rather than replaced with several new copies of the same subject.

Changing facts

Fresh does not mean guessing live values

App versions, file sizes, payment limits, processing windows, promotional amounts and account requirements can change after an article is published. TCGames.co.in will not fill those fields with invented values simply to make an article look current. When a value must be taken from the live TC Lottery screen, the article says so directly.

A date on an article means the written checks were reviewed on that date. It does not guarantee that an external platform value remains unchanged afterward. When a meaningful factual change is verified, the article can be updated in place rather than replaced by another near-identical URL.
