Agent & Referral

Agent, referral and “salary” labels may describe different things

A platform can use separate labels for referral rewards, agent commission, team activity or promotional salary-style campaigns. Do not assume they are interchangeable. Open the live agent or promotion area and identify the exact program name attached to your account.

If a public post lists a daily salary, fixed commission or guaranteed income, treat that as an unverified claim unless the same calculation and eligibility terms are visible in the account.

Agent & Referral

Verify how a referral is actually attributed

A referral normally depends on the registration source, invitation code or link recorded when the new account is created. After registration, check whether the referred account appears in the intended team or referral record before assuming future activity will count.

Keep only masked user IDs and dates for your own record. Do not collect another person’s password, OTP or payment credentials.

Agent & Referral

Team size is not the same as qualified team activity

A headline number of registered users may not equal the number that qualifies for a commission or promotion. Programs can use active-user rules, deposit conditions, valid turnover, level structures or other criteria shown in the live terms.

When a team metric looks lower than expected, compare the program’s definition of a qualified member rather than creating additional accounts to change the count.

Agent & Referral

Commission and salary claims need a visible calculation

A reliable account record should show the basis of a settlement: the relevant period, qualifying activity, rate or formula if disclosed, resulting amount and payment status. Without that record, a screenshot of a high earnings figure from another account does not establish what your own account will receive.

Avoid planning deposits or recruiting activity around a fixed income claim that is not documented in the current terms.

Agent & Referral

Check settlement history before treating a missing amount as lost

Some programs can calculate rewards on a schedule rather than instantly. Look for pending, calculated, settled, rejected or expired states and note the period attached to each. If a qualifying activity is missing, save the referred user ID in masked form, date, program level and the relevant settlement period.

Keep agent-program questions separate from ordinary game wallet results so the support request stays specific.

Agent & Referral

Do not pay outside contacts to unlock an agent account

A third party may claim they can activate a higher level, recover commission or unlock a team by receiving a private payment. Verify any fee or requirement inside the live platform before acting. Never hand over your login, OTP, withdrawal password or remote-control access to an outside helper.

If the live account does not show the claimed requirement, use the current in-account support route.

Common questions

TC Lottery Agent Program questions

Does TC Lottery guarantee a fixed agent salary?

Do not treat a fixed figure as guaranteed unless the live account shows the current program, eligibility and calculation.

Is referral commission the same as agent salary?

Not necessarily. The platform can label and calculate different programs separately.

Why can team size differ from qualified team count?

A program can apply activity, level or other eligibility conditions to decide which referred accounts qualify.

What should I save for a missing commission question?

Save the program name, settlement period, masked referred ID, qualifying status and current reward record.

Should I share my login with an agent manager?

No. Keep passwords, OTPs, withdrawal passwords and remote-access codes private.
