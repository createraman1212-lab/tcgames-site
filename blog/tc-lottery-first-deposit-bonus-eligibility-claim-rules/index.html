Bonus Eligibility

Do not assume a fixed first-deposit bonus amount

Welcome and first-deposit offers can change. The useful source is the offer card, wallet message or terms shown in the live account immediately before the deposit. If no first-deposit offer is visible, do not assume an old amount, screenshot or third-party post still applies.

Record the offer title, eligibility line, deposit range if shown, claim method, expiry and any turnover condition before making a decision.

Bonus Eligibility

Eligibility can depend on more than being a new account

An offer may be limited to a first qualifying deposit, a specific account state, a registration window, a payment route or another condition shown in the terms. Creating a new account only to repeat an offer can conflict with platform rules and can create duplicate-account problems.

If the account was already funded once, read the live eligibility status instead of assuming the next deposit counts as “first.”

Bonus Eligibility

Deposit amount and bonus amount are separate values

The money you add to the wallet and any promotional amount are different records. A deposit can complete even if a bonus is not added. Conversely, a bonus may appear in a separate promotional balance with conditions before it can be used or withdrawn.

Check the deposit order first, then the bonus or reward history. Mixing the two makes it harder to determine which part actually failed.

Bonus Eligibility

What turnover or wagering conditions mean

A turnover condition means the promotional amount may have usage requirements before some wallet actions become available. The exact multiplier, eligible game types, excluded transactions and expiry must come from the current offer terms. Do not use a number from an old post as if it were current.

If the condition is unclear, do not add more money simply to “complete” it. Read the live progress or ask in-account support to explain the account-specific status.

Bonus Eligibility

Claim button, automatic credit and pending status are different states

Some offers can require a claim action while others can credit automatically after a qualifying deposit. Follow only the flow shown in your account. If the wallet shows pending or under review, save that status and avoid repeated deposits intended to trigger the same reward.

A screenshot should show the offer title and status but hide full phone numbers, payment credentials and unrelated balances.

Bonus Eligibility

Invite code and first-deposit bonus should not be treated as the same thing

An invitation code identifies a registration source or referral state; it does not by itself prove a specific reward amount. If a reward is attached to an invitation, the live offer terms should state that separately.

Keep registration evidence and bonus evidence as separate records so an account issue can be traced clearly.
