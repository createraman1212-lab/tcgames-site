Contact TC Games

This page explains what TC Games can help with, what must be handled by the relevant platform, and how to avoid fake support contacts.

Website content feedback

TC Games accepts feedback related to factual wording, broken internal links, accessibility problems, outdated page references and unclear safety explanations.

A verified site mailbox is not currently published. When an authenticated email address is activated, it will be displayed on this page and nowhere else first. Until then, do not send personal information to addresses claiming to represent TC Games.

Account and payment support limits

TC Games cannot access user accounts, resend OTPs, reset passwords, review identity documents, trace deposits, approve withdrawals, add bonuses or change invite codes.

For those matters, use only the support option displayed inside the relevant verified platform page or app. Never rely on a phone number or social profile merely because it uses the same logo.

Information you should never send

  • Account password or one-time password.
  • UPI PIN, bank password, card PIN or CVV.
  • Full identity documents in an open message.
  • Remote-access code or screen-control permission.
  • Payment made to “unlock” support or release a withdrawal.

What a useful correction report should include

When the verified contact channel becomes available, a content report should include the exact TC Games page URL, the sentence or link involved, why it appears incorrect and any non-sensitive public source that supports the correction.

Do not include account numbers, wallet balances, full phone numbers, identity documents or private transaction screenshots.

Fake contact warning

TC Games does not currently publish a support email, messaging channel, phone number or payment account. Any person requesting credentials, remote access or money while claiming to represent this site should be treated as unverified.

Contact and support questions

Can TC Games reset my password or resend an OTP?

No. TC Games is an independent information website and cannot access or change platform accounts.

Can I send a payment reference to TC Games?

Do not send private transaction or account data. Use only the verified support option shown by the relevant platform.

Does TC Games have a verified support email or messaging channel?

No verified site email or messaging channel is currently published. Do not trust contact details that are not listed on this domain.
