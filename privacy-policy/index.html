Privacy Policy

How TC Games Handles Website Privacy

TC Games is a static informational website. It does not create gaming accounts, process payments or intentionally collect platform passwords, OTPs or wallet credentials.

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Policy scope

This policy applies to visits to tcgames.co.in and its internal pages. It does not govern third-party websites or apps opened through external links.

Technical information that may be processed

Hosting, content-delivery and security providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested URL, timestamp, referring page and security signals. This data can be used to deliver pages, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and protect website availability.

TC Games does not ask users to create a site account and does not intentionally collect gaming passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, CVVs or wallet credentials.

Cookies and local storage

The website itself does not currently use advertising or behavioural-profiling cookies. Infrastructure providers may set limited security or performance cookies when needed to protect or deliver the site.

The navigation script does not intentionally store personal data in the browser.

External links

Supporting pages may contain external Login and Register buttons. These links open a third-party destination in a new tab with privacy-protective link attributes, but the destination controls its own data collection, cookies, account processing and terms.

Review the destination address and privacy information before entering any personal or payment data.

Data minimization and retention

TC Games aims to keep only information needed for website delivery, security and error investigation. Retention periods for infrastructure logs are determined by the relevant hosting and security providers and operational needs.

Security measures

The site uses HTTPS, restrictive browser-security headers, limited script sources, no embedded payment form and static-file delivery. No internet system can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should also protect their devices and verify external domains.

Your privacy choices

You can block non-essential cookies through browser settings, clear site data, use private browsing and avoid opening external links. Blocking security-related cookies may affect access where protective checks are active.

Age and sensitive information

This website is not intended to collect information from children. Do not submit personal, financial or identity information to TC Games. Users must check applicable age and local requirements before accessing third-party services.

Policy updates and privacy contact

This policy may be updated when website features or infrastructure change. The date at the top shows the latest meaningful revision.

A verified privacy contact address is not currently published. Once activated, it will appear on the Contact Us page. Do not send data to unlisted addresses.
