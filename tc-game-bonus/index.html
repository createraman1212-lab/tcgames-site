Who qualifies

  • Offer new users, existing users ya selected accounts ke liye hai?
  • Minimum deposit or qualifying action clearly stated hai?
  • One account, one device or one payment method restriction hai?
  • Verification complete hona required hai?
  • Specific game categories excluded hain?
  • Claim button manual hai ya offer automatically credit hota hai?

Common questions

TC Game bonus questions answered

Bonus answers centered on eligibility, claim status, turnover, expiry and wallet display.

Is a TC Game bonus guaranteed for every user?

No. Eligibility can depend on account type, timing, verification, payment method and current promotional terms.

What is a turnover requirement?

It is a condition that may require a specified amount of eligible activity before bonus-linked funds can be withdrawn.

Why is my bonus not showing in the main wallet?

Some platforms separate promotional and withdrawable balances. Check the bonus section, eligibility, claim action and expiry.

Can an invite code guarantee a bonus?

No. A code may connect an account to a promotion, but amount, eligibility, turnover and expiry can change.

Should I deposit again if a bonus is pending?

Do not repeat a payment only to trigger a bonus. First save records and verify the original transaction and offer status.

What proof should I keep for a promotion?

Save the offer page, terms, timestamp, claim status and transaction reference. Never include your password, OTP or PIN in shared screenshots.

Responsible use

Treat promotional value as conditional, not guaranteed money

Uncertain reward unlock karne ke liye extra deposit, repeated payment ya borrowed money use na karein. Promotion accept karne se pehle eligibility, expiry and withdrawal conditions samjhein.
