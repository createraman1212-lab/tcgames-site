Read more than the headline amount

A TC Lottery bonus should be treated as a set of conditions, not a banner. The useful record includes the exact offer title, eligible accounts, start and end time, required action, claim method, credited wallet and any usage or withdrawal restriction. If one of those elements is missing, do not fill it in from an older promotion or another user’s screenshot.

Offer states

Visible, claimed and credited are different

Visible

The account can see an offer, but it may still require eligibility checks, a claim action or a qualifying event within the stated time.

Claimed or pending

The platform accepted a request but may still be processing it. Save the claim time and status before repeating anything.

Credited

The value appears in a named wallet. Check whether that balance has conditions different from the main or withdrawable balance.

Account-specific display

Do not assume another user’s offer applies

Accounts can display different TC Lottery offers because of creation date, activity, location, previous claims or a limited selection process. A public message showing an amount does not prove that the same offer is available to your account. Log in through the current destination and read the offer attached to the intended account before making a financial decision.

If an offer disappears, check its expiry, claim history and wallet status. Do not deposit after expiry based on the hope that support will apply an older banner. TCGames.co.in cannot view account targeting or add a missing promotion.

Codes are not interchangeable

Invitation code, gift code and account offer

Invitation code 2801498961 belongs to the supplied registration source. A gift or redeem code may be entered in a different field and can have its own validity. An account offer may need no code at all. Entering the registration code into a gift-code box does not convert it into a reward.

For every code, confirm the field label, eligible account, expiry and response. Keep the exact result—accepted, invalid, expired, already used or not eligible—without repeatedly trying codes from unknown sources.

No reward guarantee

The invitation parameter identifies a source. Only the live offer terms can establish whether a reward exists and what conditions apply.

Balance labels

Check where the credit appears

A platform can show main balance, bonus balance, promotion balance and withdrawable balance separately. Read the label instead of adding every displayed number and assuming the total can be withdrawn. Usage conditions may require specific actions before a value moves or becomes eligible for another purpose.

Missing-credit evidence

Prepare a complete promotion record

For in-platform support, record the account ID with sensitive parts masked, full offer title, eligibility wording, claim time, required action status, transaction reference if relevant and the wallet section checked. Include a screenshot of the terms and result without exposing password, OTP or full payment details.

Describe the sequence precisely: when the offer appeared, when it was claimed, what status followed and which wallet was inspected. This is more useful than stating only that a bonus is missing. Keep one support request active rather than sending different versions of the event through multiple messages.

Financial decision

Do not spend solely to chase a promotion

An offer can reduce or change the apparent cost of an action, but it does not guarantee winnings, account approval or withdrawal. Compare the required spend with the amount you were already prepared to risk. Do not increase a deposit because a countdown or message creates urgency.

Use only money that is not needed for bills, debt or essential expenses. If the complete terms are unclear, skip the offer until they can be read inside the live account.

Time and expiry

Record the offer clock before taking a qualifying action

An offer can use a calendar expiry, countdown, claim deadline or completion deadline. Identify which event must occur before the time ends: opening the offer, entering a gift code, claiming, completing a stated action or using the credited value. These are not interchangeable. Record the displayed time and timezone when present.

If an action completes close to expiry, keep its exact timestamp and reference. Do not repeat a deposit or claim merely because the promotion screen has not refreshed. Wait for the stated processing state and use platform support with the saved timeline when the result remains unresolved.

Straight answers

Bonus questions

Is invitation code 2801498961 a gift code?

It is included as an invitation code in the registration URL. A gift or redeem code can be a different code with separate conditions.

How much bonus will I receive?

No amount can be promised here. Check the offer visible inside your live account and read its current eligibility and expiry.

Can this site add a missing bonus?

No. TCGames.co.in cannot access or credit platform wallets. Save evidence and contact support inside TC Lottery.

Why is my offer different from another account?

Offers can depend on timing, account status, location or other conditions displayed by the platform.

What is the difference between claimed and credited?

Claimed means a request was accepted; credited means the value appears in the relevant wallet. The platform may show an intermediate pending state between them.

Why should I save the complete offer terms?

A title or banner alone does not show eligibility, expiry or usage conditions. The full terms establish what was displayed when the decision was made.

Can bonus balance and cash balance have different rules?

Yes. The live wallet may separate balances and place different usage or withdrawal conditions on them. Read the label and conditions attached to each balance.

What if a promotional message conflicts with my account?

Use the offer and terms visible inside the logged-in account. Do not deposit based only on an unverified message, cropped screenshot or old banner.
