Payment protection

Fake support, recovery fee and payment red flags

Never share these details

  • UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or online-banking password.
  • OTP or password sent for account or payment verification.
  • Full ID documents in public chats or comments.
  • Remote-screen access for withdrawal “release”.
  • Advance recovery fee to a personal payment account.

Keep a private record of amount, date, time, method, reference ID, displayed status and relevant screenshots. Account access issue ho to login security page follow karein.

Wallet record discipline

Deposit, withdrawal and wallet records for TC Games and TC Lottery pages

Payment pages should be handled slowly. A deposit is not complete just because money left the bank, and a withdrawal is not complete just because the platform shows a request ID. Always match wallet status, bank statement, UPI reference, amount, time and account holder details.

Before deposit, confirm the amount, recipient, minimum or maximum limit, payment method and whether the page refreshes the wallet automatically. Before withdrawal, confirm KYC or profile requirement, account-name match, fee wording, pending window and the exact destination account. Do not repeat payment just because the first status is delayed.

Payment caution

Before a second payment or support message

When money is involved, slow action is safer. If a deposit is delayed, do not immediately pay again. If a withdrawal is pending, do not share private credentials to speed it up. First collect records: amount, reference ID, timestamp, payment app status, wallet message and platform request ID.

Support conversation becomes clearer when you provide exact non-private details. A genuine process should not require UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, remote-control access or an advance recovery fee. Keep screenshots cropped so private identifiers are not exposed unnecessarily.

  • Wait for the stated processing window.
  • Compare bank and platform records.
  • Keep reference IDs but hide private credentials.
  • Reject any pressure to share PIN or OTP.

If the page refreshes during payment, do not rely only on the last screen. Open transaction history again, match the latest status and save a new screenshot. Clear records help more than repeated messages with incomplete details.

User note

Wallet action needs patience

Payment status can take time to update across bank, UPI and platform records. Keep the first reference safe, wait for the stated window and avoid opening multiple disputes with different information. Clear amount, time, status and destination details make the issue easier to review without exposing private PINs or OTPs.

Common questions

Deposit and withdrawal questions answered

Payment-specific answers for pending status, limits, recipient checks, records and verification.

How long does a TC Game withdrawal take?

Processing can vary with verification, payment method and current service conditions. Use the displayed status and stated window rather than assuming a fixed time.

What should I do if a deposit is pending?

Save the payment reference and timestamp, check the bank status and wallet history, and avoid making a duplicate payment.

Why can a withdrawal fail?

Common checks include insufficient withdrawable balance, account-name mismatch, incorrect destination details, limits or incomplete verification.

Should I pay a fee to release a withdrawal?

Do not send an advance recovery or release fee to a person or unknown account. Verify any fee only through current written platform terms.

What transaction details should I save?

Keep amount, method, timestamp, reference number, displayed status and screenshots that do not expose passwords, OTPs, PINs or full ID data.

Can bonus balance be withdrawn immediately?

Not always. Promotional balance may have eligibility, turnover, expiry or withdrawal-cap conditions that differ from cash balance.

Should I repeat a deposit if the first one is pending?

Do not repeat immediately. Save the reference ID, wait for the stated window and compare bank and wallet status first.

Why can withdrawal be delayed?

Verification, limits, payment method, account-name match and current processing conditions can affect withdrawal time.

What payment details should I never share?

Never share UPI PIN, card PIN, OTP, password, full screen access or remote-control permission with anyone.
