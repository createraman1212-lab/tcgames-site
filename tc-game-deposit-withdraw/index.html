Before a transaction

Deposit and withdrawal require different checks

Before a deposit

  • Use only the payment details generated inside the live wallet.
  • Confirm the displayed minimum, maximum and any fee.
  • Match the exact amount if the platform requires it.
  • Keep the payment reference and time.

Before a withdrawal

  • Confirm account and payment-name requirements.
  • Review the withdrawable balance, not only total balance.
  • Read the current processing window and limits.
  • Check whether identity or security verification is requested.

Support evidence

Prepare a transaction packet that can be reconciled

For a deposit, provide the masked TC Lottery account ID, amount, platform request, payment-network reference, transaction time and status shown in both places. For a withdrawal, include the request ID, amount, submission time, platform status and masked receiving-account entry. State the sequence in chronological order.

Crop screenshots to the relevant status while retaining enough context to identify the transaction. Hide unrelated balances, full bank or card numbers, OTP, payment password and identity documents. Keep one clear support conversation active and refer to the same transaction identifiers in every update.

Payment red flags

Reject instructions that bypass the live wallet

  • Do not transfer to an account sent only through a private message.
  • Do not pay an additional “unlock” or “release” fee outside the wallet process.
  • Do not share an OTP, payment password, remote-access code or full screen.
  • Do not reuse a recipient from an earlier deposit after the wallet details change.
  • Do not accept a claim that a guaranteed bonus or winning requires immediate payment.

TCGames.co.in does not collect money, control balances or employ payment agents. All transaction decisions and account support remain with the external platform and payment provider.

Account matching

Resolve a payment-name mismatch before submission

A wallet can require the platform account and payment account to belong to the same person. If the displayed name, mobile number or receiving details do not match, stop before sending money. Do not borrow another person’s payment account or edit information to imitate a match. A later withdrawal or verification review can rely on the original transaction identity.

Use the live field labels to check which name and account value must match. If a legitimate detail has changed, use the platform’s account process before a transaction rather than expecting support to correct a completed payment afterward.

Personal reconciliation

Maintain a simple wallet ledger

For each deposit or withdrawal, record the date, type, amount, platform reference, payment-network reference and final status in a private list. This makes duplicate amounts easier to distinguish and shows whether a pending entry later completed, failed or reversed. Do not rely only on temporary notifications.

Compare the ledger with the platform wallet and payment account before reporting a discrepancy. Keep screenshots only as long as needed, mask sensitive details and never share the entire ledger with an unknown person.

After completion

Confirm the final balance entry

When a deposit or withdrawal reaches a final state, check the corresponding wallet entry and the payment-provider record once more. Note whether the amount credited or received matches the request after any displayed fee. Keep the final reference until the records agree, then remove screenshots that contain unnecessary financial information.

A completed label should be evaluated against the correct account and transaction, not another payment with the same amount. The private ledger and timestamps help distinguish similar entries.

Straight answers

Payment questions

What is the minimum deposit or withdrawal?

Read the value shown in the live wallet before each transaction. Publishing a fixed number here could become inaccurate.

Why should I not repeat a pending deposit?

The first payment may still complete. A second attempt can create duplicate transactions and make reconciliation harder.

Can TCGames.co.in check my payment?

No. This site has no access to accounts, wallets, banks or platform transaction records.

What details should I keep?

Keep the amount, time, platform reference, payment-network reference and the status shown by both systems.

Why can the wallet and bank show different statuses temporarily?

The payment network and platform update independently. One may still show processing after the other changes, so compare references and wait for the stated reconciliation window.

Should I send a smaller test payment first?

Follow only the live platform instructions. A separate test transfer can fall below the minimum or create an unmatched transaction if the wallet did not request it.

What if the receiving payment details change?

Use only the details generated for the current transaction. Never reuse an account from an old screenshot, previous deposit or private message.

Why can total balance differ from withdrawable balance?

Pending activity, bonus conditions or account checks may affect the amount currently available for withdrawal. Read the label attached to each balance.
