Account protection

Password, OTP and personal information ko private rakhein

Use

  • At least 12-character unique password where accepted.
  • Device lock and updated browser.
  • Private SMS access.
  • Official recovery flow only.

Avoid

  • Same password across important accounts.
  • OTP forwarding or screen sharing.
  • Remote-access apps for “verification”.
  • Publicly posting account screenshots.

Unknown app permissions se bachne ke liye APK and permission checks bhi review karein.

Common questions

TC Game registration questions answered

Registration answers focused on mobile verification, account limits, passwords and first checks.

What mobile number should I use for TC Game registration?

Use an active number that remains under your control and can receive OTP messages. Avoid temporary or borrowed numbers.

Is OTP required for every new account?

The registration flow may vary, but mobile verification is common. Follow the current form and never share an OTP with another person.

Can I create more than one TC Game account?

Do not assume duplicate accounts are allowed. Review current platform terms because multiple accounts may create verification or payment problems.

Does an invite code guarantee a signup bonus?

No. A code may connect a referral or promotion, but eligibility, amount, expiry and turnover conditions can change.

What makes a safer password?

Use a long, unique password with mixed characters and avoid names, phone numbers, birth years or passwords reused on email and financial accounts.

What should I check after registration?

Verify the registered number, profile details, recent activity, current bonus terms and payment-account matching rules before taking further action.
